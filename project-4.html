Thunder & Silk

Neo-classical electronic, ambient techno, minimal house and saturated electro-funk. On stage first, then in the studio.

Listen →

A project in progress. A territory of its own.

Thunder & Silk is Phil Klab's current project — a set of original compositions moving through neo-classical electronic, ambient techno, minimal house and saturated electro-funk. Pieces built to move bodies as much as minds. On stage first, then in the studio.

Neo-classical piano floating over a pulsing kick. Dense textures over grooving basslines. A wide spectrum — contemplative at one end, physical and saturated at the other — with one constant thread: space as architecture, groove as language.

Thunder and silk — in the same breath.

Thunder & Silk doesn't choose a genre — it moves through several, with coherence. Each piece explores a different angle of the spectrum: the restraint of piano in a wash of reverb, the tension of a pad rising over a four-on-the-floor kick, the energy of a funk bassline taking control.

What stays constant: density. Breath. And somewhere in every piece, the moment when everything shifts.

Live — this fall

The pieces from Thunder & Silk are built for the stage first: outdoor performances in Montreal this fall, in a fully autonomous setup — synthesizers, sequencers, portable battery. Sets range from the contemplative to the physical and saturated, depending on the moment and the space.

Exact dates and locations announced in time — through the newsletter and social media.
