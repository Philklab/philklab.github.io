Current Project
The Dopamine Synth

The Dopamine Synth is a show that teaches sound design and performance through rhythm, clarity, and attitude. Each episode is short, cinematic, and precise: one idea, one sound, one payoff. Hosted by Phil Klab, it merges electronic performance, design-level visuals, and neuro-aesthetic storytelling into a modern learning experience. Learning that grooves.

Behind the sound of
Phil Klab

Founded in 2024 by Philippe Labonté (Phil Klab), this Montreal-based electronic performance project blends improvisation, design, and storytelling. After years of composing across jazz, trance, and pop, I built The Dopamine Synth — a fast, visual series where each beat teaches you something about how sound works. Whether I'm performing live or building episodes, the goal is the same: to make electronic music that moves — physically, visually, and conceptually.
