Twenty years on stage. A sound that owns itself.

Classical training, twenty years of jazz, a committed live electronic approach: Phil Klab builds a sound unlike any other — from neo-classical electronic to minimal electro-funk, atmospheric, saturated, groovy. Dense textures over grooving basslines, writing that moves between the concert hall and the intimate dancefloor.
