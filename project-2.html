PHIL KLAB

Montreal - Spring and Summer 2025

Taking over public space

Phil Klab's live electro improvisation performances were built for spontaneous deployment — metro stations, urban parks, transit spaces. 90-minute sets built entirely in real time, with a synth and a loopstation, in front of strangers with no appointment.

What it changed

These shows laid the foundation for what Phil Klab has become: an artist who composes for the live experience as much as for listening, and who believes electronic music still has something to say in public spaces.
